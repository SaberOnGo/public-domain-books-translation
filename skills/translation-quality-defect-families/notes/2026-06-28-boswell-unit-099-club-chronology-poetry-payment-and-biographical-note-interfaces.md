# Boswell Unit 099: Club Chronology, Poetry Payment, and Biographical Note Interfaces

## Scope

Use this note for Boswell/Hill annotations that mix club chronology, rival
biographer corrections, poem publication/payment evidence, and compact learned
biographies.

## Defect Family

1. Rival-biographer notes often depend on small social slights. Low-frequency
   identifiers such as `Miss Hawkins`, `Ryland`, `Payne`, and `Wright` need
   first-use source interfaces when they carry the citation chain.
2. Long club chronology notes can lose their evidentiary value if dates,
   deaths, and meeting-frequency claims blur together. Keep Johnson's letters,
   Hawkins's claim, and Hill/Boswell's inference separate.
3. Payment and copyright notes need exact book-trade language. `right of copy`,
   retained edition rights, guineas, pence-per-line, and comparative payments
   should remain numerically explicit.
4. Learned-biography notes may contain unstable source spellings or OCR-like
   Latin forms. Preserve the source form when uncertain, add a compact Chinese
   title sense only when it helps readers, and mark the instability rather than
   silently normalizing it.

## Review Checks

- Re-scan low-frequency proper nouns after the first draft; missing interfaces
  often appear in bracketed lists or citation signatures.
- For social-club chronology, build a mini timeline before approving the note.
- For quoted receipts, check every date, amount, retained right, and unit rate.
- For Latin titles in old notes, verify whether the source form is stable before
  replacing it with a normalized title.
