# Boswell Unit 098: Lexicography, Manuscript, Latin, and Dictionary Labour

## Scope

Use this note when translating Boswell/Hill annotations about Johnson's
Dictionary, manuscript transmission, source books, assistants, and later
lexicographical criticism.

## Defect Family

1. Lexicography workflow can be blurred if `amanuensis`, `clerks`,
   `copyists`, `headword`, `significations`, `definitions`, and `etymologies`
   are translated loosely. Keep the production sequence clear: mark books,
   copy quotations onto slips, sort under headwords, define senses, collect
   etymologies.
2. Manuscript citations can be mistaken for book titles. Abbreviations such as
   `MSS. Brit. Mus.` should be rendered as manuscript/archive references, not
   as a titled work.
3. Latin or foreign tags in literary notes should not be left opaque when they
   are reader-visible jokes or interpretive hinges. Keep the source form and
   add a compact Chinese gloss, especially for `Tempus edax rerum` and related
   tags.
4. Johnson's compressed critical prose often uses abstract nouns such as
   `evidence`, `refinement`, and `perspicuity` in rhetorical or argumentative
   senses. Avoid defaulting to the modern narrow sense if the sentence is about
   style or reasoning.

## Review Checks

- Reconstruct the practical workflow in Chinese and verify that the order of
  operations matches the source.
- Scan archive abbreviations and shelfmarks before deciding whether they are
  works, manuscript collections, or citations.
- For foreign-language tags, check whether the reader needs an immediate gloss
  to follow the joke or contrast.
- In dictionary-production notes, prefer terms already locked in the book
  glossary: `《英语词典》`, `词目`, `义项`, `释义`, `词源`, and `书记员`.
