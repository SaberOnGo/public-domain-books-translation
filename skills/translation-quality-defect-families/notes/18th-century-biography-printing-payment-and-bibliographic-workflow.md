# 18th-century biography: printing, payment, and bibliographic workflow

## 中文经验

- 问题族：十八世纪传记书信常把出版生产、稿酬、剧本转让、目录编制和字体规格压在同一段里，译文容易把 `copy`、`sheet`、`numbers`、`Great Primer`、`Pica`、`catalogue raisonné` 等词误作现代泛义词。
- 发现方式：优先扫描书信段、作品清单、出版计划和书目目录段中的生产词：`copy`、`sheet`、`numbers`、`guinea`、`Great Primer`、`Pica`、`catalogue`、`advertisement`、`proposals`。
- 风险：若把 `copy` 译成普通“副本”、把 `sheet` 全部译成“一张纸”、把 `numbers` 译成“数字”，读者会失去当时书籍生产和稿酬结算的证据链。
- 修复方式：先判定局部生产场景。剧本交易中的 `copy` 多指稿本或版权；按稿付酬的 `sheet` 多指印张/稿件单位；`printing in numbers` 是分册印行；`Great Primer` 和 `Pica` 是字体/字号接口；`catalogue raisonné` 应给出“评注目录”并保留源形一次。
- 复查方式：用 `rg` 扫 `copy|sheet|numbers|Great Primer|Pica|catalogue raisonné|guinea|proposals|advertisement`，再只对命中小上下文做源译对照，确认译文保留了出版生产关系而非现代泛化。

## English reusable lesson

- Defect family: Eighteenth-century biographical letters often compress publishing production, payment, play rights, catalogue work, and type specifications into one passage. Terms such as `copy`, `sheet`, `numbers`, `Great Primer`, `Pica`, and `catalogue raisonné` should not be flattened into modern generic words.
- Find it by scanning letters, work lists, publication plans, and bibliographic catalogue passages for production vocabulary: `copy`, `sheet`, `numbers`, `guinea`, `Great Primer`, `Pica`, `catalogue`, `advertisement`, and `proposals`.
- Risk: Translating `copy` as a generic duplicate, `sheet` only as a physical piece of paper, or `numbers` as digits erases the evidence chain for eighteenth-century book production and payment.
- Fix by identifying the local production scene. In play transactions, `copy` often means manuscript or rights; in payment contexts, `sheet` can mean a printed/copy unit; `printing in numbers` means instalment publication; `Great Primer` and `Pica` are type-size interfaces; `catalogue raisonné` should be rendered as a reader-readable bibliographic term with one source-form interface.
- Recheck with targeted `rg` scans for `copy|sheet|numbers|Great Primer|Pica|catalogue raisonné|guinea|proposals|advertisement`, then compare only the candidate source-target contexts to confirm the translation preserves the production relationship.
