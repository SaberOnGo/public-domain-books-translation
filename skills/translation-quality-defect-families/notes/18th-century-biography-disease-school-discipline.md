# 18th-Century Biography Disease And School Discipline Terms

Use this note when reviewing eighteenth-century English biography passages about
childhood illness, royal healing rituals, early schools, and corporal discipline.

## 中文提示

- `scrophula/scrofula` 和 `king's evil` 不要泛化成“病”或现代医学解释。
  可按语境译“瘰疬 / 王疮”，并保留它和“君主触摸疗病”民俗之间的关系。
- `regal touch` / `royal touch` 是政治、宗教、民俗医学交界处的制度性信念，
  不只是普通“触摸”。译文要让读者看见君主权威和疗病迷信同时在场。
- `black letter` 指旧式哥特体/黑字印刷，不是现代排版里的“黑体加粗”。
- `dame's school`, `usher`, `under-master`, `head-master`, `form` 等学校词要按
  早期语法学校制度处理，不要误译成现代门房、主持人、年级组管理等词。
- `rod`, `flogging`, `gallows`, `emulation` 在学校体罚论证中构成伦理链条：
  戒尺带来短期恐惧，争胜比较带来长期伤害。译文要保留这个论证结构，
  不要只把它改写成泛泛“严格教育”。

## English Note

- Keep disease names and royal-healing rituals historically situated.
- Treat school offices and class terms as grammar-school institutions, not modern
  administrative titles.
- In discipline passages, preserve the moral argument around fear, punishment,
  emulation, and sibling rivalry; do not flatten it into generic strict schooling.
