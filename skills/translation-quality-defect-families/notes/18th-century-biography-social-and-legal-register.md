# 18th-Century Biography: Social And Legal Register

Use this note when reviewing eighteenth-century English biography, especially
Boswell-style catalogue passages followed by social judgement and legal
controversy.

## 中文提示

- `intimacy` 在传记语境中常指熟稔、亲近、过从甚密，不必译成
  “亲密生活”这类容易暗示同居或恋爱关系的现代说法。
- 苏格兰法律术语要先判断制度含义，再定中文。`Vicious Intromission`
  应指对遗产事务的非法干预，不能泛化成普通“恶意干预”。
- 教会职衔和讲坛权利要保留制度色彩。`Prebendary of Westminster`
  宜译成“威斯敏斯特教堂受俸牧师”；`from the Pulpit` 可译成
  “在讲坛上”，不要只译成抽象“公开斥责”。
- 学位、学校、民法法院、议会机构和选举案也要保留制度接口。第一次自然
  出现时用词表锁定的源名接口，例如 `都柏林大学（University of Dublin）`、
  `民法法院（Commons）`、`下议院（House of Commons）`；后文再用中文译名。
  审校时用低成本扫描检查 `University`, `Commons`, `Civil Law`,
  `House of Commons`, `Master of Arts`, `Middlesex` 等触发词是否已被
  时代制度化，而不是泛化成现代大学行政、普通法院或抽象议会。
- 传记作者对前人作品的批评常兼有礼貌和锋芒。复查时要检查中文
  是否保留了“承认事实可用”和“批评误述有害”这两层关系。

## English Note

- In biographical prose, `intimacy` often means close social familiarity. Avoid
  modern wording that implies cohabitation or romance when the source does not.
- Translate legal controversy terms through the institution involved, not word
  by word. `Vicious Intromission` needs the estate/property sense.
- Keep church offices and pulpit rights institutionally legible.
- Degree, school, civil-law court, parliamentary, and election-case terms also
  need institutional interfaces. On first natural body occurrence, use the
  glossary-approved source interface, such as `都柏林大学（University of
  Dublin）`, `民法法院（Commons）`, or `下议院（House of Commons）`, then use
  the Chinese term afterward. During review, scan triggers such as `University`,
  `Commons`, `Civil Law`, `House of Commons`, `Master of Arts`, and `Middlesex`
  so they do not drift into modern university administration, generic courts,
  or abstract parliament wording.
