# 18th-Century Biography: Poetry, Religion, and Ghost-Evidence Control

## Trigger Context

Use this note when reviewing Boswell/Johnson passages that combine tavern
conversation, poetic quotation, Anglican or Christian-doctrinal language, and
supernatural testimony. These units often move quickly from anecdote to
critical judgment and then to religious epistemology.

## Defect Family

- **Poem-title and quotation flattening**: Titles such as `Elegy in a
  Church-yard`, generic `Ode`, and old-song snippets can look minor, but the
  argument may depend on the quoted lines. Translate the verse enough to
  preserve the rhetorical example, not merely the literal nouns.
- **Historic religious labels**: `high-church`, `Papists`, `Protestants`,
  `Christian revelation`, and `final causes` belong to distinct theological or
  church-political registers. Avoid casual modern paraphrase that erases the
  doctrinal contrast.
- **Ghost-evidence standard**: Johnson distinguishes imagination-produced
  experience from externally verifiable supernatural information. Keep the
  evidentiary ladder clear: imagined warning, unknown particular fact, later
  independent proof, then belief.
- **Source spelling and name collisions**: Boswell's text may vary or preserve
  old spellings. Check the book glossary before inventing new Chinese forms,
  especially for Cock-lane/Cock-Lane Ghost and repeated Johnson names.
- **Critical verb drift**: `abuse a tragedy` means harshly criticize, not
  physically abuse. `subordination` is social hierarchy or ordered ranks, not
  simple obedience.

## Review Moves

1. Confirm all note markers and quoted verse blocks against the source before
   polishing prose.
2. Search the glossary for surname-only forms as well as full names.
3. After any term repair, run a small duplicate check on `source_term` and
   `source_name`; remove accidental duplicate rows introduced during the fix.
4. In Round 1, record the family and fix it, but do not PASS. Re-read the full
   chapter in a fresh Round 2 and only PASS when the latest round has zero
   issues.
