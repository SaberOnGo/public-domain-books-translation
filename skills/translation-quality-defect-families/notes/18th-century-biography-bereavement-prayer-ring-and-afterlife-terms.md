# 18th-Century Biography Bereavement Prayer, Ring, and Afterlife Terms

## Trigger

When Boswell quotes Johnson's bereavement prayers, memorial objects, and
household testimony after Mrs. Johnson's death, the prose often shifts from
biographical narration into devotional language, Latin inscriptions, old-style
dates, and contested theological claims about the dead.

## Risk

Numeric notes can be lost in long attribution sentences before a quoted prayer.
Latin memorial inscriptions may be over-translated and lose artifact evidence,
or left untranslated and become opaque to Chinese readers. Terms such as
`conditionally`, `ministration`, `middle state`, and `eternal felicity` can be
flattened into generic feeling words, weakening Johnson's religious register.

## Low-Token Audit

- Compare numeric note sequences before and after translating every long
  attribution paragraph that introduces a prayer, letter, or manuscript object.
- Search for old-style dates, Latin abbreviations, degree signs, and short
  memorial inscriptions; decide whether to preserve the original text plus a
  Chinese gloss.
- Check devotional terms against the glossary: `ministration`,
  `conditionally if lawful`, `embodied and departed Spirits`, `middle state`,
  and `eternal felicity`.
- In domestic testimony, distinguish physical decline from moral judgment:
  phrases such as `altered for the worse` may need `大不如前` rather than
  language that sounds like blame.

## Fix Pattern

For quoted prayers, keep the Chinese solemn but readable, and do not replace
theological terms with vague consolation. For artifact text, preserve the
source inscription when it functions as evidence, then add a concise Chinese
rendering under `中文释义`. If a repair touches notes or inscriptions, record it
as a fix round and append a new full-chapter zero-issue recheck before
promotion.

## Recheck

After repairs, rerun note equality, old-marker scan, CSV width checks, coverage
checks, and an English-residue review that explicitly allows preserved Latin
artifact text. Promotion requires the latest full-chapter round to report zero
issues.
