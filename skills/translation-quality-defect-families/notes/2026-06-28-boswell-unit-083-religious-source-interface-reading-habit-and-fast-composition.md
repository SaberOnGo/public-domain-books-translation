# Boswell Unit 083: Religious source interfaces, reading habits, and fast composition

## Context

Unit 083 gathers Oxford religious testimony, Mrs. Piozzi's long anecdotal
misrepresentation, Johnson's comments on reading, and evidence about his rapid
composition. The recurring risk is that compact eighteenth-century source
phrases can sound harmless while producing stiff Chinese or losing citation
interfaces.

## Defect Family

- Religious source-name gaps: Whitefield, Wesley, Law, Gibbon, devotional
  works, church names, and journals often appear in dense notes; each still
  needs the approved first source interface when important.
- Latin motto flattening: a phrase such as `Stet pro ratione voluntas` should
  receive a target-language sense before or beside the source form.
- Reading-habit abstraction drift: claims about reading books "through" or
  works by "mere man" should become natural Chinese, not source-shaped phrases
  such as `单由人写出`.
- Publication-speed term drift: `octavo pages`, `as the moment pressed`, and
  "written on Saturday" belong to printing and deadline context, not vague
  page-size or time-pressure literalism.
- Anecdote quotation stiffness: Piozzi's bracketed sarcasm and Boswell's
  correction should remain legible as argument, not become a pile of literal
  clauses.

## Audit Pattern

1. Scan source triggers:
   `Whitefield|Wesley|Mr. Law|Christian Perfection|Serious Call|Gibbon|La Bruyere|Stet pro ratione voluntas|mere man|read it through|octavo pages|moment pressed|Pr. and Med|St. Marie`.
2. Scan target residues:
   `单由人写出|宗教曾从我的心中脱落|应付给我的钱|八开本后的四十八页|时刻催迫|墨菲《约翰生》（Murphy`.
3. Check whether devotional works and biography sources have readable Chinese
   titles before source forms, and whether source forms appear only where the
   reader needs identification.
4. For fast-composition anecdotes, compare printing-unit words against the
   source line before choosing a page-size term.

## Fix Pattern

- Use setting-3 source interfaces for important note-level names and works:
  `乔治·怀特菲尔德（George Whitefield）`, `劳先生（Mr. Law）`,
  `《基督徒完全》（Christian Perfection）`, and similar forms.
- Pair Latin mottoes with a Chinese sense, for example `让意志代替理性`
  before `Stet pro ratione voluntas`.
- Rebuild compact English abstractions into idiomatic Chinese:
  `仅由凡人写出`, `从我的心中消失`, `发稿时限催迫`.
- Render printing units explicitly enough for readers:
  `四十八个八开本印刷页`, not a vague "after octavo" phrase.

## Recheck

The latest PASS round should have matching note numbers, no source-page or
editorial abbreviation residue, no target residues from the audit list, and a
target-only reading that keeps the religious and publication-history notes
clear without over-explaining them.
