# English Titles and Typography / 英文标题与排版

Use concise, meaningful English titles. Choose title case or sentence case once per book; section numbers remain navigable and do not consume first body occurrences of names. Titles and navigation use target forms without first-mention source annotations. Preserve meaningful puns or ambiguities when possible; document alternatives in QA, not in a long navigation label.

Use English commas, periods, colons, semicolons, question marks, and quotation marks. One normal interword space; no letter spacing for ordinary prose. Use curly or straight quotes consistently. Em dashes and ellipses express the source's rhetoric, not decoration. Do not import Chinese list commas into English.

For reflowable English prose, use an ordinary first-line indent around 1.5em, modest line height around 1.5-1.65, and no simultaneous large paragraph gaps. First paragraphs after headings may be unindented. Keep body fonts reader-controlled, images at max-width:100%, and table text wrappable. Source-language bilingual blocks retain their own script-appropriate layout; the English target stays at normal body size.

记录标题大小写、段首缩进和英文标点约定；语义标题最多三级。手机窄屏须核对长标题、图注和表格。字体、颜色及字号应允许阅读器覆盖。
