# English Quality Standard / 英文质量标准

This is a project acceptance standard, not a claim of an external accreditation.
这是项目验收标准，不宣称外部认证。

1. Fidelity: preserve every source proposition, action sequence, causal link, comparison, qualification, negation, number, speaker, and intentional uncertainty. Do not add psychology, explanations, zoological corrections, or moral commentary to make the source more plausible.
2. Natural English: use idiomatic verb phrases, articles, prepositions, clause order, and collocations. Resolve references when the source context establishes them; retain deliberate ambiguity. Avoid comma splices, dangling modifiers, false possessives, and abstract noun scaffolding.
3. Voice: preserve genre, narrative distance, emotional temperature, register, recurring imagery, and purposeful repetition. Do not make all dialogue colloquial or all narration lyrical. Violent or distressing material retains the source's force without added sensationalism or sanitization.
4. Rhythm: vary sentence length according to the source's narrative pressure. Rebuild source syntax without breaking causal relations or turning scenes into fragment lists. Strong actions use precise verbs; explanatory additions must not substitute for images.
5. Terminology: distinguish fixed identity/technical terms from context-dependent ordinary words. Preserve accurate species, anatomy, units, institutions, and cultural references. Do not substitute a familiar but different species or institution. Dictionary-level equivalence alone is insufficient.
6. Names: follow the locked book registry and selected display policy. A translated meaningful name must remain an identity, not become a generic description; spelling and capitalization stay stable. Source spellings appear only where policy or a recorded linguistic discussion permits.
7. English convention: record US/UK spelling and quotation style in the book profile and apply them consistently. Use normal English punctuation, apostrophes, word spacing, and dialogue paragraphs. Do not reproduce source fullwidth punctuation in English prose, except the locked proper-name first-display interface if the renderer requires it.
8. Reader text: headings, captions, alt text, notes, book information, navigation, metadata, and body prose all require English review. Notes are restrained and separately identified. QA, prompts, model labels, raw placeholders, and production instructions never enter body text.

每段均须完整忠实且自然；重建句法不等于增删信息。保留有意重复、叙事距离和情绪强度，禁止擅加动机、科学纠错或道德评语。高风险术语应有术语表、禁用译法及上下文证据。

Chapter acceptance requires all dimensions in `templates/evaluation_rubric.md` to meet their threshold, a fresh zero-issue whole-chapter control, and independent hash-bound semantic evidence. Final random review uses the common excellence line: each agent average >=92, lowest >=88, all sampled rows scored, zero blocking issues. Scores never override a confirmed omission, addition, misunderstanding, or unresolved literary debt.
