# English Evaluation Rubric / 英文评分表

| Dimension | Full-quality evidence | Minimum /5 |
|---|---|---|
| Fidelity | Complete meaning, voice, uncertainty, actions, causal links | 5 |
| Natural English | Finished idiomatic prose, no source-syntax reconstruction needed | 5 |
| Voice and register | Genre and narrator preserved without invention | 4 |
| Rhythm and imagery | Clear action, appropriate repetition, precise images | 4 |
| Terminology and names | Exact identities, locked register, stable senses | 5 |
| Reader interfaces | Clear titles, notes, captions, alt text, navigation | 4 |
| Defect closure | Confirmed families audited, repaired, rechecked, backfilled | 5 |

Vetoes: missing or invented information; wrong actor, relationship, number, negation, species or technical sense; unexplained source residue; broken notes; reader-confusing prose; unresolved polysemy; unsupported polish. A candidate scoring 80-87/100 needs revision. Final random review requires each reviewer average >=92 and minimum >=88, with specific scored evidence per sample. Do not award identical scores by template or infer quality from a successful build.

一票否决项包括漏译、增译、指代/否定/数字/物种错误、读者难解、未决多义词及无依据润色。分数不能覆盖已确认缺陷。
