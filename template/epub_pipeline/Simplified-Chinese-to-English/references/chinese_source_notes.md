# Chinese Source Interpretation / 中文源文判读

- Omitted subjects: reconstruct only when paragraph context establishes the actor; distinguish an animal's individual action from the pack's action. Repeated names can be better than an ambiguous English pronoun.
- Topic chains and serial verbs: identify actor, time, cause, manner, and result before writing English clauses. Do not retain comma chains or invent temporal links.
- Aspect and tense: 了/过/着 encode different functions, not automatic past/perfect/progressive replacements. Lock narration tense; shift for direct speech, habitual claims, recollection, and explanatory present only when context supports it.
- Idioms, reduplication and sound: translate scene function, force and register, not an unexplained word-for-word idiom. Preserve audible or visual effects where meaningful; avoid nursery-rhyme tone in tense action.
- Measure words and numbers: retain quantities and physical scale. Distinguish individual animals, litters, groups, ranked positions and ages. Do not translate approximate Chinese quantities as exact values.
- Names: distinguish real personal/place names, meaningful fictional names, nicknames and generic descriptions. Use established English spellings where verified; meaningful fictional names may use a stable semantic rendering supported by context. Do not manufacture a meaning for a phonetic name.
- Animal and technical words: distinguish canine teeth from generic teeth, pelt/fur from hide/skin, claws from paws, estrus from mating, prey from carrion, and species from generic animal classes. Verify unfamiliar senses against source context and reliable references.
- Anthropomorphism: preserve the authorial portrayal of ambition, emotion and motives as narration. Do not rewrite fiction into field biology, nor import fictional claims as verified science in editorial notes.
- Culture and source history: retain cultural imagery and narrative framing; add a short note only when necessary. Source factual anomalies go into textual QA and an explicit reader-note decision, never silent correction.
- Normalization: preserve raw EPUB bytes and original Unicode. Record corrections to OCR/spacing, old TOC, commercial boilerplate or link artifacts; retain actual author/edition matter, notes, appendices and meaningful illustrations.

以上规则针对中文源文，不能机械照搬其他语言的干扰清单。每个疑义必须绑定当前段、整章与可用后文；局部已可判清的不能推给审校。
